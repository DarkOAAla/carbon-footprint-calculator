Carbon Footprint Calculator

Climate change is real!!

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases, mainly carbon dioxide, that our daily activities release into the air. Driving, using electricity, eating and shopping all add to it, and together they are warming our planet.

Do you know how much carbon you produce?

Small changes in how we live can protect the Earth for future generations. Find out your footprint today and see where you can make a difference.
